# JoyFlix 🎬

JoyFlix is a modern, privacy-focused, and extensible media streaming application for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV.

---

### Features:
- **100% Ad-Free**: No ads, no popups, no trackers.
- **Privacy First**: No account required, zero telemetry.
- **Modern Streaming UI**: Clean hero banners, fluid playback, and AMOLED dark design.
- **Android TV & Phone**: Optimized for touchscreens as well as TV remote controls.
- **Chromecast & Subtitles**: Full subtitle download, audio sync, and cast support.
- **Extensible Architecture**: Compatible with custom streaming extensions.

---

### Getting Started:
1. Open JoyFlix on your Android device.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Extensions** to add your preferred extension repositories.
3. Browse and enjoy!
